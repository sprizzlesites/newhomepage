Install

Two lines

The stylesheet points at fonts/ with relative paths, so keep the folder together. GitHub Pages serves .woff2 correctly with no configuration.

<link rel="stylesheet" href="sprizzle-pack/sprizzle.css">

<h1 class="sprizzle" data-text="Sprizzle!">Sprizzle!</h1>

Three fonts stack to make the wordmark — cyan edge underneath, dark contour, pink body on top. The upper two are drawn by pseudo-elements, which is why the text is mirrored into data-text. One line fills that in for you:

<script>
  document.querySelectorAll(".sprizzle").forEach(function (el) {
    el.dataset.text = el.textContent;
  });
</script>

Also in the pack

Keyed artwork

sprizzle-sheet.png is your original sheet with the white keyed out — counters included — and glyphs/ holds all 92 characters as separate transparent PNGs, in case you want the original raster somewhere the font can't go.

Artwork and letterforms are yours. The outlines here were traced from sprizzle-sheet.png; build-1-trace.py and build-2-fonts.py regenerate the whole pack from the sheet.
